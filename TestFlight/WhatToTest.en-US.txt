Thanks for trying Jamaal.

New in this build, please try:

1. Look back. On Today, the Yesterday row opens a read-only look at yesterday: what you did, what you moved, your Anchors and habits. Use the arrows to go to earlier days. If you forgot to tick something, you can tick a missed task for two days.
2. Look ahead. The Coming up row lists tasks you dated for later, by day. They stay off Today until their day.
3. Change a task where it stands. Open any task and change its effort, how much it matters, its day, its category or its title. There is no Save button: it saves as you go, and an Undo appears for a few seconds when you leave. If a task repeats, Jamaal asks whether a change is for just that one or for every one after.

Also worth a go: add a few tasks, make a habit and an Anchor, and plan tomorrow in the evening. Add the Today and Next Anchor widgets (on the Lock Screen too). If you use more than one device, check that changes show up on the others.

This beta is the full app with nothing to pay for and no trial to count down.

Tell me about anything that confuses you, or that closes the app. The easiest way: Settings, then iCloud sync, then Email support. It opens a message to support@jamaal.app with some technical details filled in (never your tasks, notes or places), and you add what happened above them. You can also email support@jamaal.app directly.
