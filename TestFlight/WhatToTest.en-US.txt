Thanks for trying Jamaal.

New in this build, please try:

1. A calmer top of Today. The tasks now sit near the top. The capacity slider, your Anchors and your habits each fold to one line: tap the chevron beside "Medium · 2h 15m of 3h" to change your capacity, and tap the Anchors or Habits line to open the full list. Jamaal remembers what you left open. The wellbeing strip has moved to the Wellbeing tab.
2. A next-gap line. When there is a free stretch before your next Anchor and a task that fits it, a line above your tasks says so: "Free until Asr · 1h 40m. Fits: …". Tap it to open that task. If there is nothing useful to say, it stays out of the way. (It uses each task's effort estimate, so tasks with no estimate are never suggested.)
3. A steadier order. When two tasks are due the same day, the one that matters more comes first, then the one you keep putting off, then the shorter one. A task due later never jumps ahead of one due today.

Still worth a go from last time: the Yesterday row (a read-only look back, and you can tick a missed task for two days), the Coming up row (tasks you dated for later), and changing a task where it stands (no Save button; an Undo appears when you leave; a repeating task asks whether a change is for just that one).

Also: add a few tasks, make a habit and an Anchor, and plan tomorrow in the evening. Add the Today and Next Anchor widgets (on the Lock Screen too). If you use more than one device, check that changes show up on the others.

This beta is the full app with nothing to pay for and no trial to count down.

Tell me about anything that confuses you, or that closes the app. The easiest way: Settings, then iCloud sync, then Email support. It opens a message to support@jamaal.app with some technical details filled in (never your tasks, notes or places), and you add what happened above them. You can also email support@jamaal.app directly.
