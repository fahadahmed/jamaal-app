On the desktop, macOS renders widgets vibrant (monochrome) while another app is in front, so the accent grouping matters here too. Notification Center uses the full-colour versions at the same sizes. Pointer hover adds nothing; click opens the Today window.

iPad
The same small, medium and large frames: iPad widget sizes are larger in points, and the layouts are built on flexible stacks, so they gain air rather than content. Large still shows five tasks. On the iPad Lock Screen the medium appears in a column and follows WG-05's redaction. A task tap on a wide layout opens jamaal://task/<id> beside the list.
