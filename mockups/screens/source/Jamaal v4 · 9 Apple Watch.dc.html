Apple Watch

A companion that does four things: shows the next task, the open Anchor window, the day's habits and a running focus session, and lets you act on each with one tap or a turn of the crown. The iPhone owns the data; the watch queues what you do. Frames are 46 mm (208 × 248 pt), shown at 1.5× for review. Register IDs WA-01 to WA-08 are new.

A full-screen cover, not a push. While a session runs, swiping down shows the pages underneath with a small timer pill at the top that leads back. A session started on the iPhone appears here too.
Launch rule
1. A session is running → Focus. 2. An Anchor window is open and unmarked → Anchors. 3. Otherwise → Today. After an hour away, it doesn't reopen where you left off; it follows the rule again.
Every action
Shows at once, queues to the iPhone, and keeps a dashed mark until the phone confirms (WA-06). No screen waits on the phone, and none shows a spinner.
Dead ends, by design
No add, defer, drop, planning, Wellbeing or settings. Where someone might look for them (the task detail), one line says More on your iPhone and the system's handoff picks it up.
First run
No onboarding on the watch. Before the first snapshot arrives, Today says Open Jamaal on your iPhone to begin. Read-only users see the pages without action buttons.

How it works
iPhoneOwns the store and the engine. Builds WidgetSnapshot after every save.
snapshot →
latest wins← actions
queued, idempotent
WatchReads the last snapshot. Shows marks at once; sends each action with an id and a time.
Not on the watch: Night Planning, Wellbeing, Settings, adding a task, Defer and Drop. Notifications for window-closing and the evening mirror from the iPhone; Mark done becomes a notification action.
