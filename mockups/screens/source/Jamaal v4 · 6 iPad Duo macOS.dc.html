iPhone Duo · two size classes, bars to the side

HIG · "Raise the bar" tech talk · product page: outer 5.36″, inner 7.58″, same aspect ratio
Size classes, not poses
Outer display = compact width, inner = regular width. No fixed widths or breakpoints; layout margins and horizontal safe-area insets do the work. Both displays share one aspect ratio (≈ √2), so each half of the open display is the size of the outer one — the frames here are drawn at ≈ 476 × 673 pt and ≈ 952 × 673 pt, estimated from the published diagonals; confirm against Apple Design Resources. The hierarchy and the feature set are identical inside and out, so opening the device never changes what's possible.
One vertical region on the right
On the outer display, the status bar, toolbar and tab bar share one column on the right, fixed to the hardware (it doesn't flip in RTL; content does). Order: close/back, the prominent action, other groups, spacer, tabs. Content is offset from the column, not centred on the display. System TabView and NavigationStack/SplitView toolbars only.
Overflow and priority
Today is navigation-led, so the toolbar compresses before the tabs (the default). Add is high visibility priority; Filter and Plan tomorrow move first into the system ellipsis menu. Items stay symbol-only in the bar but always carry a title.
Stays horizontal
The session chip carries real information (the running time), so it stays a horizontal accessory in the content. On the inner display, sheets always use horizontal bars. The note format bar stays attached to the keyboard.
