Habits and Anchors

Habits are read by density, never streaks. Anchors are read by attendance, and only an open window can be attended. Also redrawn here because the brief reworks them: HB-01, HB-02 (streak row removed) and the HB-03 type picker. HB-04 and HB-05 keep their v3 drawings.

Counted with a stepper. Glasses of water, dhikr.

Same rules in both: a slip is logged when it happens; a day is full only if slips stay within the allowance and the day was engaged; otherwise it stays empty. No "days since" counter. The options differ in whether holding is something you say (A) or something the app infers from the day (B).
