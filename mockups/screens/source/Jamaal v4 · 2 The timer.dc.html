The chip
Lives in the system tab bar's bottom-accessory slot, so it is on every tab and moves with the bar on Duo. The capsule is glass (an action surface); the digits sit in a small chip that is filled terracotta only while the clock is live — paused, it goes back to outline.
The numeral
Display role at its largest step (38, Fraunces 300) with tabular digits, per the tokens page — it grows with Dynamic Type rather than being drawn huge. Overrun changes nothing but the words beside it.
Haptics
Done is one soft impact. Pause, Stop for now, Defer and Drop are silent. Nothing vibrates when the estimate passes.
