Night Planning

Five steps — review, carry forward, build tomorrow, check the load, close — with Skip tonight on every one. A full-screen modal on the phone; one canvas with a step rail on wide (NP-07). Nothing is self-reported: the mood line is gone.

{{ themeLabel }}
The example evening
Monday 20:05, planning Tuesday. Working day 08:00–19:00, normal day 3h, Tuesdays default to medium. Tuesday holds the school run, a site visit, Dhuhr, the dentist and Asr.
Deferral vs rescheduling
Carry forward (step 2) moves tasks whose day has arrived — real deferrals. Pushing tomorrow's task out (step 3) and the overload Move (step 4) are rescheduling: no count, no easing.
One filled button
Continue is the terracotta on every step. Per-task choices (Keep · Later · Drop, Move) are outline or selected-fill, so the step's one decision stays the obvious one.
