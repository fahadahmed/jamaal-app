A task is something you do when you can. An Anchor is different: its time is set by something outside you. The school gates open when they open. The bins go out on the night they go out.

## What an Anchor is

Every Anchor has a **window**: a time it begins and how long it stays open. Dhuhr might open at the call to prayer and stay open until Asr. The school run might be a half-hour around three.

You don't tick it off. You say what happened to it, and there are three honest answers:

- **Attended.** You made it.
- **Not today.** You chose to skip it.
- **Someone else did it.** Someone else did it for you.

If the window closes without an answer, Jamaal reads it as a plain fact, "window closed at 15:30", not a failure. And if you did do it after all, you can mark it done later that same day.

## Rules, and one-offs

Most Anchors come from a **rule**: certain days of the week, every few days or weeks, or "a few days after the last time", which suits watering plants. A rule can have more than one time in a day, like drop-off and pick-up, and Jamaal makes the Anchors for today and tomorrow for you.

For something that happens once, like a dentist visit, add a **one-off Anchor** from the same plus sheet you use for tasks, and switch it to Anchor.

## Prayer times

If prayer times matter to you, Jamaal can set them up as Anchors: you choose which prayers, and it works out the times from where you are. It asks for your location only while the app is open, and only for this; the location stays on your device. You can also pick a place by searching for a city, and choose a calculation method that suits your region. There is a longer guide for [Salah, Qur'an and dhikr](/journal/salah-quran-dhikr).

## Taking a break

An Anchor rule can take a break, for a trip or a season, without losing the rule itself. It goes quiet and comes back when you say so.

## On Today and on your Lock Screen

Anchors sit at the top of Today, sorted by when they open. A rule with several times appears as one tidy row, with a count of how many you've attended. The Next Anchor [widget](/journal/widgets-and-the-lock-screen) shows the one that's open, or the next to open, on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.
